# Tout

> Tout (ToutApp) was a sales engagement platform — email tracking, templates, and analytics for sales teams — and a 500 Global portfolio company. It was acquired by Marketo in 2017 and became Marketo Sales Connect; toutapp.com now redirects to the Marketo Sales Connect page on business.adobe.com. Tout no longer operates a standalone API or developer surface.

## Company

- [Website (redirects to Adobe/Marketo Sales Connect)](https://toutapp.com)

## APIs

No standalone Tout APIs are published. The historical ToutApp developer surface (api.toutapp.com) is offline.
